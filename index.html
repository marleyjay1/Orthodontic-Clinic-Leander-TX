Comprehensive Orthodontic Care in Leander

Orthodontics is about more than simply creating a straighter smile. Proper tooth and jaw alignment can contribute to improved oral function, easier cleaning, and a healthier-looking smile. An orthodontic evaluation can help identify concerns such as tooth crowding, spacing, overbite, underbite, crossbite, and other alignment issues.

At SmileCrew Orthodontics, treatment begins with understanding the patient's individual needs. The process can include an initial consultation, treatment planning, starting braces or aligners, routine appointments, treatment completion, and retainers.

Braces for Children, Teens, and Adults

SmileCrew offers traditional metal braces, ceramic braces, and self-ligating braces. Ceramic braces can provide a more discreet appearance, while traditional braces remain a dependable option for many orthodontic cases.

Clear Aligners and Invisalign Options

For patients who want a less noticeable orthodontic treatment, clear aligners may be an attractive option. Clear aligners are removable and designed to gradually move teeth toward a healthier alignment.

SmileCrew identifies Invisalign as one of its treatment options and provides clear aligner care for eligible patients. Because every smile is different, an orthodontic consultation is important for determining whether aligners are suitable for a specific case.

Clear aligners may be particularly convenient for adults and teens who prefer a discreet treatment option and appreciate the ability to remove their aligners when eating or maintaining their oral hygiene.
